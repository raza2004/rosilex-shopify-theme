free for commercial use, no attribution required. They are not part of the theme
(this folder is in `.shopifyignore`). Upload them to products in Shopify admin under
Products > (product) > Media.

These are texture and mood shots, not photos of our products. Use them as the
second image on a product (the card hover shows it) until real packshots exist.
Customers need to see the actual product as the first image.
